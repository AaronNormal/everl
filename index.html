Questions

Is Everl a dating app?

It can be, but most people use it to meet others for things: karaoke, padel, a pub quiz or a drink. You choose Social or Adult when you join, and you only see people who chose the same.

Do I need a profile or photos?

No. Everyone uses a codename like “Ochre Wren 641”. Nobody sees your name or face until you both accept and meet.

How do you keep people safe?

Everyone is age-verified once, you only meet in public places that Everl picks, and every meeting has a safety contact you can alert by holding a button.

Is it free?

Yes. Meeting people is free. Everl+ adds planning ahead and more than one meeting at a time.

When is it coming to my area?

Manchester and Leeds open first, one neighbourhood at a time. Join the waiting list and we’ll email you when yours opens.
